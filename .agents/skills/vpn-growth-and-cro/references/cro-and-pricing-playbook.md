## 2. The Reinvestment Governance Formula

Never increase daily ad spend blindly. Govern budget scaling on a **7-Day Rolling Cash-Flow Accounting Cycle**:

$$
\text{Next Week Daily Ad Budget} = £10.00 + \frac{\left(\text{Gross Cash Collected}_{7\text{d}} - \text{Payment Fees}_{5\%} - \text{Refund Reserve}_{7\%} - \text{Infra Reserve}_{13\%}\right) \times \text{Reinvestment Rate}}{7}
$$

- **Gross Margin After Direct Variable Costs:** **~75%** (5% Stripe/Crypto processor fee, 7% refund reserve, 13% bare-metal 10Gbps server bandwidth reserve).
- **Recommended Reinvestment Rate:**
  - **Stage 1–2 (Months 1–2):** **70%–80% of Net Contribution** reinvested into paid traffic + content tooling.
  - **Stage 3–4 (Months 3–6):** **60%–70% of Net Contribution** reinvested into paid scaling, retaining 30%–40% as founder profit/treasury reserve.
- **Circuit Breaker Rule:** If 7-day rolling Paid CPA exceeds **£26.00** (or Day-1 Cash ROAS drops below **1.15x**), freeze budget increases for 72 hours, pause the lowest-performing ad group, and rotate creative angles.
