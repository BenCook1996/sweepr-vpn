# VPN Product Marketing & Growth Context (`AGENTS.md`)

This workspace contains the marketing, SEO, digital advertising, and conversion optimization operating system for selling and scaling our VPN product starting from a **£10/day (£300/month)** base budget with an automated **revenue-reinvestment flywheel**.

## Core Strategic Principles

1. **Avoid the "Red Ocean" Head Terms Early**: Never spend micro-budget paid media or early domain authority chasing broad head terms like *"Best VPN"* or *"Buy VPN"* (£10–£20+ CPC, Keyword Difficulty 90+). Target high-intent technical, problem-aware, and niche use-case segments first.
2. **Strict Ad Platform Compliance**: Ad networks (Google Ads, Reddit Ads, Meta, Microsoft) aggressively flag VPN ads under *"Circumventing Systems"* or *"Dishonest Behavior"* if ad copy or landing pages claim to bypass copyright/geo-blocks (e.g., *"Unblock US Netflix"*). Always frame paid creatives around **cybersecurity, public Wi-Fi encryption, zero-logs privacy, ISP throttling protection, low-latency routing, and open-source protocol transparency (WireGuard/OpenVPN)**.
3. **Day-1 Cash Flow Positive Pricing (Anchor Architecture)**: To make a £10/day budget compound via reinvestment, you cannot wait 6 months for monthly £8 subscriptions to pay back a £25–£35 CAC. Use **Anchor & Decoy Pricing** (with international currencies equated and adjusted to 1 pence/cent/unit under a round number):
   - **Monthly Plan (Flexible Decoy):** £9.99/mo ($12.99 USD | €11.99 EUR | CA$17.99 CAD | A$19.99 AUD | CHF 10.99 | ¥1,999 JPY) — plus £1.99 ($2.99 / €2.99) 1st-Month Intro Offer
   - **6-Monthly Plan (Mid-Tier Anchor — Save 35%):** £38.99 / 6 months (£6.50/mo equivalent | $50.99 USD | €46.99 EUR | CA$69.99 CAD | A$77.99 AUD | CHF 42.99 | ¥7,799 JPY)
   - **Yearly Plan (Core Cash Cow — Save 50%):** £59.99 / year (£5.00/mo equivalent | $77.99 USD | €71.99 EUR | CA$107.99 CAD | A$119.99 AUD | CHF 65.99 | ¥11,999 JPY)
   - **SWEEPR Wi-Fi Radar & Smart-TV Shield Add-On:** +£14.99/yr | +£9.99/6mo | +£1.99/mo
   Upfront 6-month and annual cash collection produces immediate >1.5x Day-1 ROAS, unlocking rapid daily budget scaling.
4. **Trust Over Corporate Polish**: Privacy buyers distrust flashy corporate claims. Lead with verifiable technical proof: live server load/latency stats, WireGuard config generators, transparent privacy policy, warrant canary, independent or reproducible build verification, and anonymous/frictionless checkout (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Stripe, and Crypto/Monero).
