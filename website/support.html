If standard UDP 51820 is blocked on university eduroam, NHS guest Wi-Fi, or hotel networks, switch to our Stealth UDP 443 endpoint inside the SWEEPR VPN client or set interface MTU = 1380 (use 1412 on UK PPPoE fibre and 1420 on Ethernet).
