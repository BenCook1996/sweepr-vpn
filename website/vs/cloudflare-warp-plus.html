Cloudflare WARP+ Technical FAQ

Why does Cloudflare WARP+ omit manual city or country selection?

Cloudflare routes WARP+ using global BGP anycast and Argo congestion metrics, which always terminate at the nearest colocation facility. If you specifically need a fixed UK, German, Swedish, or US egress node for SSH allowlists or stable peering, you need a city-selectable VPN like SWEEPR.

Can I export a BoringTun or MASQUE key from WARP+ for GL.iNet or OpenWrt?

Not officially. While unofficial third-party scripts attempt to scrape WARP registration keys, Cloudflare relies on its native client for MASQUE and posture telemetry. SWEEPR natively generates standard WireGuard .conf files in your dashboard for one-paste router deployment.
