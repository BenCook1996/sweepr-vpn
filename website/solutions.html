Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my ISP is throttling my evening internet speed?

Run our free 10-second ISP Throttling Test at https://sweeprvpn.com/tools/isp-throttling-test between 19:00 and 23:00. If unencrypted CDN/streaming streams drop below 25 Mbps while a 10Gbps WireGuard tunnel achieves 850+ Mbps, your ISP is applying SNI/DPI traffic shaping.

What WireGuard MTU should I use for my ISP?

Use MTU 1420 for standard Cable/DOCSIS and DHCP FTTP (Virgin Media, Hyperoptic, Community Fibre, Comcast, Rogers), MTU 1412 for PPPoE FTTP (BT, Sky, TalkTalk, Plusnet, Zen, Vodafone FTTP), and MTU 1380–1400 for 5G/LTE CGNAT and Campus/Hotel Wi-Fi (EE, Three, O2, Eduroam).
