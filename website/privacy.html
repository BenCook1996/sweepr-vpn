Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: November 2026 | Version 2.6 • Cross-check our Official Brand Fact Sheet and Technical Privacy FAQ.

1. User Data We Collect (And Our Strict Zero-Logs VPN Distinction)

Yes, we collect limited user data on our website (sweeprvpn.com), Customer Portal, and licensing servers. We practice strict data minimization: while our VPN tunnel operates under a strict zero-logs policy for your internet traffic, we collect the following limited account, billing, and website data required to operate your subscription:

  • Account, Licensing & Authentication Data: When you create an account, subscribe to SWEEPR VPN, or sign in to the Customer Portal (/login), we collect your email address, subscription plan tier, one-time passcode (OTP) login verification records, and a one-way cryptographic device seat hash used solely to enforce your 3 simultaneous devices limit.
  • Billing, Currency & Payment Data: When you subscribe, we collect your billing country, currency preference, and transaction status. All payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe, Inc., PayPal, Google Play Billing, or Apple App Store (pre-launch — not yet live) under PCI-DSS Level 1 encryption—SWEEPR never sees or stores your full credit card number or CVV.
  • Website Analytics, Cookies & Attribution Data: When you visit our public website (sweeprvpn.com), we collect aggregate website usage telemetry (pages viewed, referral source, browser/device type, approximate country derived from IP address, and anonymized storefront/download CTA clicks via POST /api/telemetry and POST /api/tools/telemetry) via first-party session storage and Google Analytics 4 (G-7D28EEMNXX) as disclosed in Section 4—strictly separated from our zero-logs WireGuard VPN tunnel.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: When you contact SWEEPR VPN Support via our Support Center or email, we collect your name, email address, and message contents to resolve your request.
  • What Our VPN Tunnel & Wi-Fi Scanner Never Collect: While connected to SWEEPR VPN, we never log, monitor, record, or store the websites you visit, your DNS queries, your source IP address mapped to browsing sessions, or the contents of your encrypted WireGuard traffic. Furthermore, our Home Wi-Fi Device Scanner runs 100% locally on your device and never uploads your local Wi-Fi device list to our servers.

2. What We Collect vs. Never Log: Connection State in RAM vs. Billing Record Separation

SWEEPR VPN physically separates its stateless WireGuard relay nodes from its billing and licensing database. Below is the exact engineering boundary between ephemeral tunnel state and commercial account records:

Connection State (Volatile RAM / tmpfs Only)
Active WireGuard peer handshakes (Curve25519 / ChaCha20-Poly1305) and tunnel IP leases (10.64.0.2/32) exist solely in volatile tmpfs memory across our 8 bare-metal POPs and are purged on disconnect or reboot.
Never Logged (Zero Disk / /dev/null)
Browsing history, destination IPs, traffic payloads, and in-tunnel DNS queries (10.64.0.1) are never written to disk; systemd-journald packet logging is disabled and DNS query logs route to /dev/null.
Billing & Licensing Record Separation
Email address, plan tier, payment processor status (Stripe, PayPal, Google Play; Apple StoreKit 2 pre-launch / not yet live), and one-way cryptographic seat hashes (enforcing 3 simultaneous devices + unlimited router .conf exports) live in an isolated billing database separated from WireGuard relay nodes.
Local Home Wi-Fi Scanner & Smart-TV ACR Shield
ARP/mDNS/SSDP LAN discovery runs 100% locally on your device without uploading your Wi-Fi table; the 65-domain Smart-TV ACR blocklist runs via static DNS sinkhole rules with zero per-user query logging.
Website & Storefront Telemetry (sweeprvpn.com only)
Google Analytics 4 (G-7D28EEMNXX, disclosed in Section 5) plus PII-stripped first-party CTA/tool/uninstall diagnostics (/api/telemetry, /api/tools/telemetry, /api/uninstall-feedback) execute only on public web pages—never inside the VPN tunnel or native client apps.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the user data we collect exclusively to:

  • Provision and authenticate your encrypted WireGuard VPN credentials and Ed25519-signed license tokens across up to 3 simultaneous devices.
  • Process subscription payments, issue tax/billing receipts, and send transactional login codes or renewal notifications.
  • Display accurate regional pricing (GBP, USD, EUR) and measure aggregate website traffic and campaign conversions on sweeprvpn.com.
  • Respond to customer support requests and maintain network uptime.

4. How We Use Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Automated Threat Detection

Yes, we use Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine-assisted heuristic systems across our network security lists, server load balancing, and customer support operations:

  • AI-Assisted Malicious Domain & Tracker Threat Feeds: Our threat-intelligence pipeline uses AI-trained domain classifiers to identify newly registered phishing domains, malware command-and-control hosts, and Smart TV ACR telemetry endpoints, compiling them into static DNS blocklists enforced at the VPN and local filter layer.
  • Automated Smart Server Routing & Local Wi-Fi Device Identification: We use automated heuristic scoring to route connections to the lowest-latency WireGuard relay node (using aggregate, non-identifiable server load metrics) and local on-device OUI/heuristic matching to identify unknown devices on your home Wi-Fi network.
  • AI-Assisted Customer Support & Diagnostics: We may use AI-assisted tools internally to triage support tickets, draft setup guides (such as router and gaming console WireGuard configurations), and diagnose aggregate connectivity issues.
  • Strict AI Privacy Guarantee (Zero AI Training on VPN Traffic): We never inspect your VPN tunnel traffic, never send your browsing activity or DNS queries to external AI providers, and never use customer personal data or network traffic to train or fine-tune external AI or large language models (LLMs).

5. Third-Party Data Collectors, Analytics & Named Sub-Processors

Yes, we use third-party data collectors and infrastructure sub-processors on our website (sweeprvpn.com) and billing backend. Inside our VPN tunnel and Windows, Linux, Android, and macOS desktop client applications—as well as our upcoming pre-launch (not yet live) native iOS/iPadOS/macOS App Store build—there are zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero cross-app tracking SDKs (App Tracking Transparency tracking is disabled / not used), and zero behavioral trackers. On our website, checkout, and licensing infrastructure, we share or process limited data only with the following named third parties:

  • Google LLC (Google Analytics 4 — Measurement ID G-7D28EEMNXX): Third-party website analytics collector used strictly on sweeprvpn.com web pages to measure aggregate website traffic, page views, session engagement, and checkout conversions (not loaded inside native App Store apps or embedded in-app views with ?app=1). You may opt out at any time using browser tracker blocking or the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on.
  • Apple Inc. (App Store & StoreKit 2 Billing — Pre-Launch): Our native iOS, iPadOS, and macOS App Store application and StoreKit 2 in-app purchase flow are currently pre-launch (not yet live). Once released, Apple will process App Store subscription purchases and renewals and SWEEPR will receive only signed Apple transaction verification metadata (originalTransactionId, product identifier, and renewal status), never your Apple ID password or payment card details.
  • Stripe, Inc., PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A. & Google Play Billing: PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processors and billing providers that collect and process your payment method, billing country, subscription status, and fraud-prevention signals during web or Google Play checkout.
  • First-Party Storefront & Download Click Telemetry (/api/telemetry & /api/tools/telemetry): Strictly on sweeprvpn.com web pages, we log anonymized, PII-stripped outbound store and installer CTA clicks (cta, platform, source_page, target) directly to our first-party backend. This website telemetry never runs inside the WireGuard VPN tunnel or client applications.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. & Railway Corp.: Cloud infrastructure, Content Delivery Network (CDN), DDoS mitigation, TLS 1.3 termination, and backend application/licensing hosting providers.
  • Country.is (api.country.is) & GeoJS (get.geojs.io): Lightweight IP-to-country lookup services used on our website pricing page solely to display your local currency (GBP, USD, or EUR).

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third-party marketers.

6. Data Security, Retention, Account Deletion & Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK DPA & CCPA)

We protect your account and licensing data using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, and Ed25519 cryptographic signatures. Account and billing records are retained only for the active duration of your subscription plus statutory UK/EU tax compliance periods. Under the EU GDPR, UK Data Protection Act 2018, CCPA, and Apple App Store guidelines, you have the right to access, rectify, export, opt out of website analytics cookies, or permanently delete your account data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion (Desktop & Pre-Launch Apple App Store Builds): Open Account → Delete Account & Data inside the SWEEPR VPN app (live on desktop; included in our pre-launch iOS/iPadOS/macOS App Store build) to immediately revoke all linked WireGuard device seats, erase your local Keychain/configuration, and permanently delete your server account record.
  • Customer Portal Deletion: Sign in at /login or email support@sweeprvpn.com to request immediate deletion.
