Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last updated: November 2026 | Version 2.5

1. User Data We Collect (And Our Strict Zero-Logs VPN Distinction)

Yes, we collect limited user data on our website (sweeprvpn.com), Customer Portal, and licensing servers. We practice strict data minimization: while our VPN tunnel operates under a strict zero-logs policy for your internet traffic, we collect the following limited account, billing, and website data required to operate your subscription:

  • Account, Licensing & Authentication Data: When you create an account, subscribe to SWEEPR VPN, or sign in to the Customer Portal (/login), we collect your email address, subscription plan tier, one-time passcode (OTP) login verification records, and a one-way cryptographic device seat hash used solely to enforce your 5-device simultaneous connection/seat limit.
  • Billing, Currency & Payment Data: When you subscribe, we collect your billing country, currency preference, and transaction status. All payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe, Inc., PayPal, Google Play Billing, or Apple App Store under PCI-DSS Level 1 encryption—SWEEPR never sees or stores your full credit card number or CVV.
  • Website Analytics, Cookies & Attribution Data: When you visit our public website (sweeprvpn.com), we collect aggregate website usage telemetry (pages viewed, referral source, browser/device type, approximate country derived from IP address, and marketing conversion events) via first-party session storage and the third-party analytics/attribution tools disclosed in Section 4.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: When you contact our support team via our Support Center or email, we collect your name, email address, and message contents to resolve your request.
  • What Our VPN Tunnel & Wi-Fi Scanner Never Collect: While connected to SWEEPR VPN, we never log, monitor, record, or store the websites you visit, your DNS queries, your source IP address mapped to browsing sessions, or the contents of your encrypted WireGuard traffic. Furthermore, our Home Wi-Fi Device Scanner runs 100% locally on your device and never uploads your local Wi-Fi device list to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the user data we collect exclusively to:

  • Provision and authenticate your encrypted WireGuard VPN credentials and Ed25519-signed license tokens across up to 5 devices.
  • Process subscription payments, issue tax/billing receipts, and send transactional login codes or renewal notifications.
  • Display accurate regional pricing (GBP, USD, EUR) and measure aggregate website traffic and campaign conversions on sweeprvpn.com.
  • Respond to customer support requests and maintain network uptime.

3. How We Use Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Automated Threat Detection

Yes, we use Artificial Intelligence (AI) and machine-assisted heuristic systems across our network security lists, server load balancing, and customer support operations:

  • AI-Assisted Malicious Domain & Tracker Threat Feeds: Our threat-intelligence pipeline uses AI-trained domain classifiers to identify newly registered phishing domains, malware command-and-control hosts, and Smart TV ACR telemetry endpoints, compiling them into static DNS blocklists enforced at the VPN and local filter layer.
  • Automated Smart Server Routing & Local Wi-Fi Device Identification: We use automated heuristic scoring to route connections to the lowest-latency WireGuard relay node (using aggregate, non-identifiable server load metrics) and local on-device OUI/heuristic matching to identify unknown devices on your home Wi-Fi network.
  • AI-Assisted Customer Support & Diagnostics: We may use AI-assisted tools internally to triage support tickets, draft setup guides (such as router and gaming console WireGuard configurations), and diagnose aggregate connectivity issues.
  • Strict AI Privacy Guarantee (Zero AI Training on VPN Traffic): We never inspect your VPN tunnel traffic, never send your browsing activity or DNS queries to external AI providers, and never use customer personal data or network traffic to train or fine-tune external AI or large language models (LLMs).
