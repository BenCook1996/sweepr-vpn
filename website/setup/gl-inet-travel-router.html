1. Repeater Wi-Fi Setup & Captive-Portal MAC Cloning Walkthrough

Hotels, trains, and airport lounges intercept unauthenticated HTTP traffic on a captive portal page before granting internet access. If WireGuard is active or DNS is forced prematurely, that splash page cannot load. Follow this sequence in the 192.168.8.1 admin UI:

  1. Temporarily leave VPN → WireGuard Client turned OFF and set Block Non-VPN Traffic off until authentication finishes.
  2. Open Internet → Repeater → Scan, pick the hotel SSID, and connect. If the splash portal opens automatically on your laptop browser, accept the hotel terms.
  3. If the portal refuses to render behind a router, sign in on your smartphone first, note your phone's Wi-Fi MAC address (disable Private Wi-Fi Address for that SSID), then open Network → MAC Address → Clone on the GL.iNet router and paste your phone's MAC before disconnecting your phone from the hotel SSID.
  4. Once hotel bandwidth flows, slide your GL.iNet physical toggle switch to engage SWEEPR WireGuard and turn Block Non-VPN Traffic back on.

2. Binding the Physical Toggle Switch & Tuning MTU = 1380

Under System → Toggle Button Settings, assign the hardware slide switch to WireGuard Client. Flipping that switch physically tears down or restores your encrypted tunnel in under two seconds—handy when clearing airport logins before re-enabling full protection.

Dropping MTU = 1380 accommodates nested 4G/5G carrier GRE/CGNAT headers and strict hotel firewall encapsulations without fragmenting HTTPS packets. On home fibre links, raise it back to 1420 (or 1412 on PPPoE). Notice port 443 UDP helps traverse restrictive hospitality networks that drop default port 51820.

GL.iNet Travel Router Questions

Why does the hotel login page time out when WireGuard is toggled on?

Hospitality splash portals sit on an unroutable local gateway before internet routing unlocks. Toggle the physical switch off, accept the hotel portal, then flip WireGuard back on.

Should I enable GL.iNet AdGuard Home alongside SWEEPR 10.66.0.1 DNS?

Either disable AdGuard Home or configure its upstream resolver exclusively to 10.66.0.1 inside the tunnel so DNS queries never leak onto the hotel subnet.

Can I power Beryl AX directly from a USB-C laptop port or power bank?

Yes—GL-MT3000 draws roughly 4–6W under full Wi-Fi 6 repeater load and runs smoothly off any standard 5V/3A USB-C PD battery pack.
