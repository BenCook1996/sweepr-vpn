macOS Apple Silicon WireGuard FAQ

Does WireGuard conflict with macOS iCloud Private Relay or AirDrop?

When a system-wide WireGuard tunnel is active, Safari honors the VPN route instead of Apple's Private Relay proxy, while AWDL peer-to-peer AirDrop continues functioning locally over direct Wi-Fi links.

Why should I disable On-Demand on my trusted home SSID?

If you already run SWEEPR on your home OpenWrt, UniFi, or ASUSWRT-Merlin router, excluding your home SSID prevents double-encapsulating traffic inside a second WireGuard header.

How do I keep Time Machine NAS backups working while tunneled?

Check Exclude Private IPs inside the tunnel preferences (or keep RFC1918 ranges out of AllowedIPs) so Bonjour .local Time Machine SMB mounts remain on your local LAN.
