How This Free Internet Throttling Test & ISP Speed Slowdown Checker Works

      

        Direct Answer: Standard speed tests (like Ookla Speedtest) connect to whitelist servers hosted inside your ISP's own core network, showing fast headline speeds even while your 4K streaming, gaming, or downloads are throttled. Our free internet throttling test measures round-trip latency variance (jitter), HTTP chunk timing, and Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) exposure across real external transit paths.
      

      

        

          
1. SNI & DPI Video Classification

          
Between 19:00 and 23:00, ISPs inspect unencrypted TLS Server Name Indication (SNI) headers (e.g., googlevideo.com, nflxvideo.net, ttvnw.net) and shape sustained video streams. SWEEPR VPN encrypts the entire packet payload with 10Gbps WireGuard.

        

        

          
2. CGNAT & UDP Queue Starvation

          
Broadband and 5G providers using Carrier-Grade NAT (100.64.x.x) deprioritize high-packet-rate UDP gaming and WebRTC calls. Tunneling over SWEEPR VPN's 4ms UK-LON-01 or 8ms NL-AMS-01 nodes bypasses retail CGNAT queues.

        

        

          
3. Calibrated MTU (1420 / 1412 / 1380)

          
Many legacy VPNs slow down fibre connections because they leave WireGuard at an uncalibrated MTU. SWEEPR VPN automatically matches your path MTU (1420 Cable/DHCP, 1412 PPPoE FTTP, 1380 5G) for zero packet fragmentation.
