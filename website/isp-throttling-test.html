How This Free Internet Throttling Test & ISP Speed Slowdown Checker Works

      

        Direct Answer: Standard speed tests (like Ookla Speedtest) connect to whitelist servers hosted inside your ISP's own core network, showing fast headline speeds even while your 4K streaming, gaming, or downloads are throttled. Our free internet throttling test measures round-trip latency variance (jitter), HTTP chunk timing, and Deep Packet Inspection (DPI) exposure across real external transit paths.
      

      

        

          
1. SNI & DPI Video Classification

          
Between 19:00 and 23:00, ISPs inspect unencrypted TLS Server Name Indication (SNI) headers (e.g., googlevideo.com, nflxvideo.net, ttvnw.net) and shape sustained video streams. SWEEPR VPN encrypts the entire packet payload with 10Gbps WireGuard.

        

        

          
2. CGNAT & UDP Queue Starvation

          
Broadband and 5G providers using Carrier-Grade NAT (100.64.x.x) deprioritize high-packet-rate UDP gaming and WebRTC calls. Tunneling over SWEEPR VPN's 4ms UK-LON-01 or 8ms NL-AMS-01 nodes avoids retail CGNAT queue starvation.

        

        

          
3. Calibrated MTU (1420 / 1412 / 1380)

          
Many legacy VPNs slow down fibre connections because they leave WireGuard at an uncalibrated MTU. SWEEPR VPN automatically matches your path MTU (1420 Cable/DHCP, 1412 PPPoE FTTP, 1380 5G) for zero packet fragmentation.

ISP Throttling & Speed Shaping FAQ

      

        
How do ISPs detect and throttle 4K streams?
Broadband providers inspect unencrypted DNS lookups, TLS SNI hostnames, and high-bitrate UDP signatures during 18:00–23:00 peak hours to deprioritize video, P2P, and gaming flows.

        
Does WireGuard stop ISP bandwidth shaping?
When all DNS queries and payloads travel inside an encrypted WireGuard interface with fq_codel pacing and Stealth UDP 443 fallback, your ISP sees only opaque UDP packets.

        
How does this ISP throttling test compare unencrypted vs VPN speeds?
Compare your ping and throughput first on raw broadband and again immediately after connecting to SWEEPR VPN — a jitter drop proves middlebox DPI prioritization was active.

        
Which MTU stops bufferbloat during uploads?
Set MTU = 1412 on PPPoE fibre (BT/Sky/Plusnet), 1420 on DOCSIS/DHCP cable (Virgin Media), or 1380 on 5G/Starlink CGNAT.
