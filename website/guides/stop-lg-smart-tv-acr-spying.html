1. Exact LG webOS Remote Menu Path to Disable Live Plus & Home Ads

Start inside webOS settings (webOS 22, 23, 24, and 25 on LG C2/C3/C4/G4 OLED and QNED sets) so the local Live Plus screenshot daemon stops hashing HDMI inputs:

  1. Disable Live Plus ACR: Hold the Settings cog button on your Magic Remote, open General → System → Additional Settings → Live Plus, and switch it Off.
  2. Turn Off Sponsored Home Shelves: Under General → System → Additional Settings → Home Settings, disable Home Promotion and Content Recommendation.
  3. Limit Ad Tracking & User Agreements: Navigate to Support → Privacy & Terms → User Agreements, uncheck Viewing Information, Voice Information, and Interest-Based Advertising, then select Limit Ad Tracking under Additional Settings → Advertisement.

2. Why ibs.lgappstv.com & cdpbeacon.lgtvcommon.com Persist After Opt-Out

Even when Live Plus is toggled off in the UI, LG's launcher firmware continues contacting cdpbeacon.lgtvcommon.com, homeprv.lgtvcommon.com, and nudge.lgtvcommon.com on startup to populate carousel tiles and measure interface engagement, alongside ad-call probes to ibs.lgappstv.com and ad.lgappstv.com.

Does disabling Live Plus stop cdpbeacon.lgtvcommon.com home-screen pings?

No. Turning off Live Plus only stops HDMI pixel fingerprinting. LG webOS still fetches sponsored tile configurations and telemetry from cdpbeacon.lgtvcommon.com and nudge.lgtvcommon.com unless you also turn off Home Promotion and sinkhole those domains at the router DNS layer.

Which LG domains should stay allowed so the LG Content Store and app updates work?

If you sinkhole ibs.lgappstv.com and ad.lgappstv.com, keep snu.lge.com and ngfts.lge.com (or update servers selectively) accessible when pulling new webOS app binaries, and avoid blocking playback CDNs like Netflix, Disney+, BBC iPlayer, or YouTube.

Why did my LG OLED prompt me to re-accept Privacy Terms after an update?

LG bundles revised User Agreements inside webOS OTA updates. If a household member clicks Select All to dismiss the screen quickly, Live Plus re-activates immediately—making router-level sinkholing at 10.64.0.1 essential for persistent protection.
