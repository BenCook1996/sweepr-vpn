1. Why gpm.samsungqbe.com & samsungacr.com Keep Talking After Opt-Out

Samsung's SyncPlus engine operates directly inside the Tizen display driver, capturing visual signatures across terrestrial TV, HDMI consoles, and built-in apps. Even when users reject Viewing Information Services during setup, Smart Hub background daemons still resolve gpm.samsungqbe.com, samsungcloudsolution.com, and config.samsungads.com roughly once per minute to fetch remote configuration flags and promoted shelf slots.

Whenever Samsung pushes a seasonal Tizen OS firmware update, revised Terms & Privacy notices frequently reappear at power-on, risking accidental opt-in from anyone holding the remote.

2. Step-by-Step Tizen Menu Hardening & PSID Reset

Complete these exact menu changes on 2023–2026 Samsung Tizen Smart TVs before applying your router DNS rules:

  1. Open Terms & Privacy: Press Home on your Samsung SolarCell remote and open Settings → All Settings → General & Privacy → Terms & Privacy → Privacy Choices.
  2. Uncheck Viewing Information Services: Deselect Viewing Information Services (Tizen's SyncPlus ACR tracker), Interest-Based Advertisements, and Voice Recognition Services (if unused).
  3. Reset Your PSID: Inside Privacy Choices → Reset PSID, reset your Personalized Service ID so any previously associated ad-profile token is invalidated locally.

Samsung Tizen Privacy Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Samsung PSID and why should I reset it after turning off Viewing Information Services?

Samsung's Personalized Service ID (PSID) ties your Smart Hub app launches and ACR viewing profiles to a persistent advertising identifier. Resetting it inside Privacy Choices → Reset PSID wipes that historical identifier while router sinkholes prevent a new profile from uploading.

Why does gpm.samsungqbe.com still appear in my router query logs after opting out?

Samsung Smart Hub uses gpm.samsungqbe.com for telemetry configuration polling and home-bar messaging every 60 seconds regardless of ACR opt-out toggles. Sinkholing the domain at 10.64.0.1 drops the heartbeat completely.

Will blocking samsungcloudsolution.com stop Tizen firmware updates or Samsung apps?

No. Tizen firmware binaries are served from otn.samsungcloudcdn.com (or can be flashed via USB), while Netflix, BBC iPlayer, YouTube, and Disney+ use independent streaming CDNs untouched by ACR blocks.
